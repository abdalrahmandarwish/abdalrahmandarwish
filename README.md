## Selected work
**[Sales and order reporting in Power BI](https://github.com/abdalrahmandarwish/power-bi-sales-analysis)**  
I analyzed 23,603 sales lines across 1,465 orders. The report connects five KPIs to breakdowns by time, territory, product and shipping method.

**[Market basket analysis in Python](https://github.com/abdalrahmandarwish/market-basket-analysis)**  
I applied Apriori to 7,501 grocery baskets and ranked product combinations by lift. The notebook and results show how to weigh a strong association against the number of baskets that support it.
